# Category guide

The category names in the sheet (`Categories` column A, down to the TOTAL row) are the source of truth. If the user has renamed or added one there, use the sheet's version. The form lines follow recent versions of the US forms; the user checks them against the current year's forms.

The household's own rules in `My situation` always beat this guide.

| Category | Goes on | Typical receipts | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advertising & marketing | Schedule C line 8 | Domains, ads, business cards, portfolio hosting | |
| Car & truck | Schedule C line 9 | Fuel, parking, tolls, repairs for business driving | With Standard mileage, fuel and repairs are not counted separately: ask. Parking and tolls for business trips still count. |
| Contract labor | Schedule C line 11 | Payments to freelancers and subcontractors | |
| Equipment (depreciation / Sec. 179) | Schedule C line 13 | Computers, monitors, phones over $2,500 per item | Always `Needs answer`. See questions.md. |
| Insurance | Schedule C line 15 | Business liability or equipment insurance | Health and home insurance do not go here. |
| Legal & professional fees | Schedule C line 17 | Accountant, lawyer, tax preparation for the business | |
| Office expenses | Schedule C line 18 | Postage, printer ink, paper, small office items | |
| Rent / lease | Schedule C line 20 | Coworking desk, studio, rented equipment | Rent for the home itself is a home shared cost. |
| Supplies | Schedule C line 22 | Items up to $2,500 each used for the work: keyboard, cables, materials | |
| Taxes & licenses | Schedule C line 23 | Business licences, permits | Income tax payments do not go here. |
| Travel | Schedule C line 24a | Flights, hotels, transport on overnight business trips | Ask for the purpose of the trip if the rules do not cover it. |
| Meals (50%) | Schedule C line 24b | Meals with clients or while travelling for work | The sheet halves it. Write the full printed total. |
| Utilities | Schedule C line 25 | Utilities for a separate business location | Household utilities are a home shared cost. |
| Phone & internet | Schedule C line 27a | Mobile plan, home internet | Business use % comes from `My situation` B9 and B10. Blank there means ask. |
| Software & subscriptions | Schedule C line 27a | SaaS, AI tools, cloud hosting, trade publications | Streaming and personal apps are personal. |
| Education & training | Schedule C line 27a | Courses, technical books, conference tickets | |
| Home office | Form 8829 / line 30 | Things bought for the office room itself: desk lamp, shelves, paint | Only if `My situation` B11 is Yes. With the Simplified method these are not deducted: still write the row, and say so in Notes. |
| Other | Schedule C line 27a | A clear business cost that fits nowhere else | Say what it is in Notes. |
| Home shared costs | Form 8829 | Rent, household utilities, renter's or homeowner's insurance | Leave Business use % blank; `Filing summary` applies the office share. Only if B11 is Yes and B12 is Actual expenses. Otherwise write it as personal and say why in Notes. |
| Dependent care | Form 2441 | Daycare, after-school care, day camp | Only for the people and providers named in `My situation` B18. |
| Personal (not deductible) | Not counted | Groceries, clothing, anything in "always personal" | Business use % is `0`. Status is `Suggested`. |

Supplies or Home office? An item used for the work itself (keyboard, tablet case) is Supplies. An item that fits out the room (shelf, draft stopper) is Home office.
